Missing telemetry

The spatial view may hold the last received pose for context, but chart paths break across missing intervals and the inspector reports that no candidate sample exists. The gap is not interpolated.

The included runs and 200,000-sample validation are synthetic. This is replay and analysis software, not a robot controller, SLAM system, or certified safety system.
