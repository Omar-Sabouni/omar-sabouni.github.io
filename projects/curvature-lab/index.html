Curvature Lab

A browser tool for selecting a point on one of four analytic parametric surfaces and inspecting its local curvature.

The torus, sphere, hyperbolic paraboloid, and catenoid each provide analytic first and second derivatives. The app derives the fundamental forms and reports Gaussian curvature K, mean curvature H, and principal curvatures k₁ and k₂.

What changes with the selected point

  • The mesh can be colored by Gaussian, mean, minimum-principal, or maximum-principal curvature.
  • A marker and unit normal locate the selected surface point.
  • The u and v controls provide a precise alternative to selecting on the WebGL canvas.

Run

npm install
