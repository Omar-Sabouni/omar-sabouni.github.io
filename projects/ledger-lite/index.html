Ledger invariant

Decimal strings enter one PostgreSQL transaction. Balances are derived from immutable rows, every transaction has signed postings that sum to zero, and corrections append an exact compensating reversal. Account creation and money movement require idempotency keys; multi-account transfers lock accounts in a consistent order.
