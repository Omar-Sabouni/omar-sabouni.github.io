InspectFlow

Conveyor inspection that runs classical OpenCV rules and a small CNN on the same frames and scores every gate decision against ground truth.

Held-out scenes
20
CNN parameters
145k
Automated tests
91

Both detectors feed the same tracker and inspection gate. The CNN was trained in PyTorch on synthetic frames, exported to ONNX, and runs on CPU through OpenCV DNN.

In poor light the classical color threshold rejects most good parts, and the darkest parts are never segmented. The CNN never saw that lighting during training and decides every part correctly, at about ten times the CPU cost per frame. Flat-field correction raised the classical detection recall but not its accuracy.

All scenes are synthetic. The perfect CNN score shows that it learned this simulator. It has not been tested on camera footage.
