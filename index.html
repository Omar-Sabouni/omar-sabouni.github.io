Projects

  1. InspectFlow

    InspectFlow checks parts on a simulated conveyor with OpenCV rules and with a small CNN I trained in PyTorch. In poor light the rules make the right call on 46% of parts and the CNN on all of them, at 18 ms a frame on one CPU core.

  2. Robot Replay Studio

    Robot Replay Studio is a TypeScript and Three.js tool that runs in the browser and keeps a 3D robot scene, incidents, timeline, and signal plots on one replay clock. Ten detectors are covered by 141 tests and a 200,000-sample validation run.

  3. TelemetryLink

    TelemetryLink is a dependency-free C++20 streaming decoder that keeps partial frames and recovers after noise, invalid lengths, or CRC failures. It covers 30 parser edge cases and a two-million-frame synthetic benchmark.

  4. Curvature Lab

    Curvature Lab lets you pick a point on one of four parametric surfaces and see its Gaussian, mean, and principal curvatures.
