Projects

  1. InspectFlow

    A Python and OpenCV conveyor inspection pipeline that tracks parts, records gate decisions, and replays annotated runs in the browser. Nominal and poor-lighting fixtures keep measurement behavior and undecided cases visible.

  2. Robot Replay Studio

    A browser-local TypeScript and Three.js tool that keeps a 3D robot scene, incidents, timeline, and signal plots on one replay clock. Ten detectors are covered by 141 tests and a 200,000-sample validation run.

  3. TelemetryLink

    A dependency-free C++20 streaming decoder that preserves partial frames and recovers after noise, invalid lengths, or CRC failures. It covers 30 parser edge cases and a two-million-frame synthetic benchmark.

  4. Curvature Lab

    A browser tool for selecting a point on one of four analytic parametric surfaces and inspecting its Gaussian, mean, and principal curvatures.
